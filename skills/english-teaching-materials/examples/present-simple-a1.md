# Ukázka – Present Simple, A1

**Brief:** WS, 15–17 let, učňovská škola, A1. Téma: daily routine. Cíl: žák sestaví kladnou a zápornou větu, položí otázku a napíše dvě věty o své rutině. 15 minut, A4.

## Pro žáky: My daily routine (15 minutes)

**1. Choose (3 min).** Circle the correct answer.
a) He **get / gets** up at six.
b) I **don't / doesn't** watch TV in the morning.
c) **Do / Does** she work on Saturdays?

**2. Complete (4 min).** Fill in the gaps.
a) My friend ___ (go) to school by bus.
b) We ___ (not play) football every day.
c) ___ you ___ (study) English on Mondays?

**3. Make sentences (4 min).** Write one positive and one negative sentence about Sam.
Facts: Sam – get up at 7; Sam – not eat breakfast.
a) _____________________________________
b) _____________________________________

**4. About you (4 min).** Write two true sentences about your routine and ask your partner one question.
a) _____________________________________
b) _____________________________________
Question: _______________________________

## Pro učitele: klíč
1: a gets, b don't, c Does.
2: a goes, b don't / do not play, c Do – study.
3: *Sam gets up at seven. Sam doesn't eat breakfast.*
4: Otevřené odpovědi – posuzuj tvar, význam a srozumitelnost.
